# Company Newsletter

## Instructions
You are helping write a company-wide newsletter. Company newsletters are periodic communications (weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly) that keep all employees informed about what's happening across the organization.

The goal is to create a scannable, engaging summary that:
- Celebrates wins and milestones
- Keeps everyone aligned on company direction
- Highlights important announcements
- Recognizes team and individual contributions
- Shares upcoming events and deadlines

## Tools Available
You should use the company's available tools to gather content:
- Slack: Major announcements, team wins, shoutouts from leadership
- Google Drive: Strategic docs, presentations, important memos
- Email: Executive communications, company-wide announcements
- Calendar: Upcoming all-hands, company events, important deadlines
- Press/External: Any external mentions, awards, or recognition

## Workflow

1. **Determine scope**: What time period does this cover? Weekly? Monthly?
2. **Gather content**: Use available tools to find newsworthy items
3. **Organize by section**: Group similar items together
4. **Write headlines**: Make each section scannable
5. **Add context**: Brief explanations where needed
6. **Review tone**: Should be upbeat, inclusive, and professional

## Formatting

Structure the newsletter with clear sections:

```
# [Company Name] Newsletter - [Date/Period]

## Highlights
- [Top 2-3 most important items]

## Team Updates
- [Department/team wins and progress]

## People
- [New hires, promotions, anniversaries, shoutouts]

## Announcements
- [Policy changes, important deadlines, reminders]

## Upcoming
- [Events, milestones, things to look forward to]

## In the News
- [External press, awards, recognition - if applicable]
```

## Tone Guidelines
- Professional but warm
- Celebratory without being over-the-top
- Inclusive - represent different teams and roles
- Scannable - use bullet points and short paragraphs
- Action-oriented - clear next steps where applicable
