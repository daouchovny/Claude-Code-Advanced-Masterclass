# General Internal Communications

## Instructions
You are helping write an internal communication that doesn't fit into the other specific categories (3P updates, newsletters, FAQs). This could include:

- Status reports
- Leadership updates
- Project updates
- Incident reports
- Policy announcements
- Team announcements
- Meeting summaries
- Decision documentation

## Discovery Process

Before drafting, gather the following information:

1. **Audience**: Who will receive this communication?
   - All employees? Specific team? Leadership only?

2. **Purpose**: What should happen after people read this?
   - Awareness only? Action required? Feedback needed?

3. **Tone**: What emotional register fits?
   - Formal/official? Casual/friendly? Urgent? Celebratory?

4. **Format**: Any specific structural requirements?
   - Company template? Character limits? Platform constraints?

5. **Context**: What background do readers need?
   - What do they already know? What's new information?

## Core Principles

When writing internal communications:

- **Clarity first**: Lead with the most important information
- **Be concise**: Respect people's time
- **Active voice**: "We decided" not "It was decided"
- **Action-oriented**: Clear next steps if any are required
- **Inclusive**: Consider all audiences who will read this
- **Link to sources**: Reference docs, previous comms, or context

## Workflow

1. **Clarify the ask**: What type of communication and for whom?
2. **Gather context**: Use available tools or ask user directly
3. **Draft structure**: Outline before writing
4. **Write draft**: Follow principles above
5. **Review**: Check tone, clarity, and completeness

## Tools Available
- Slack: For context on discussions, decisions, team sentiment
- Google Drive: For source documents, previous communications
- Email: For related threads and prior context
- Calendar: For event details, attendees, meeting notes

## Default Format

If no specific format is requested:

```
**Subject/Title**: [Clear, descriptive title]

**TL;DR**: [1-2 sentence summary]

**Context**: [Brief background - why are we communicating this?]

**Details**: [Main content]

**Next Steps**: [If applicable - what should people do?]

**Questions?**: [Where to direct questions or feedback]
```
